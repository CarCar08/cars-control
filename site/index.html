C.A.R.S. Control

One Windows app to update, uninstall, clean up and keep an eye on everything on your PC, instead of a pile of separate tools. Free and open source.

Download for Windows Source code User guide

Windows 10 (2004+) and 11, 64-bit. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen asks once: why, and how to check the download.

What it does

Updates, all of them

Apps from winget and the Microsoft Store, Windows Update, WSL, PowerShell modules, npm and pip, checked together and installed with one button.

Uninstall properly

Bulk uninstall with a review of the leftovers it finds; leftovers go to a quarantine you can restore from for 30 days.

Find the space

A treemap of each drive, big and old files, duplicates, junk, games you don't play, moving games and folders to another drive.

Health that explains itself

Drive wear and errors read from the drives themselves, temperatures, blue-screen analysis in plain words, freezes, sleep and boot times, drivers.

Privacy switches with undo

Telemetry, ads and AI extras, each showing what's set right now, why you might change it, and exactly how to switch it back.

Backups and your setup

Versioned backups to a cloud folder, a USB drive or another disk, and your preferences kept in step across PCs.

A closer look

Careful by design

  • Nothing happens until you say so. Every page measures first and shows you the list; risky items start unticked, and most have a dry run.
  • Runs as you. Only a small helper gets administrator rights, once per session, and it accepts a fixed list of checked operations, never commands or paths.
  • Unattended work never pops up a prompt. Scheduled jobs that need admin wait for your approval.
  • No telemetry. Bug reports are something you send yourself, after reading them. Everything it connects to.
  • Explanations everywhere. Tooltips, (?) explainers and Recommended choices for your kind of PC.
